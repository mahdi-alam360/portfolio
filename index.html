Better alignment.
Same architecture.

Improving self-supervised medical image registration through a two-phase training strategy: multi-resolution curriculum learning and adaptive optimization.

+1.7 points

Up to 1.7-point improvement in Dice Similarity Coefficient across public medical-image datasets.

PythonSelf-supervised learningOptimization
Project documentation on GitHub ↗
Read the engineering breakdown +

The problem

Align medical images while evaluating both overlap accuracy and deformation quality.

The approach

Extended the On-the-Fly Guidance framework with a coarse-to-fine training curriculum and adaptive optimization, without changing model architecture or inference pathways.

The evaluation

Ran reproducible experiments, ablation studies, and benchmark comparisons on LPBA40, IXI, and OASIS. Used Jacobian determinant analysis to evaluate deformation quality.

The related manuscript was listed as in preparation in the supplied resume (2025).
